# Provenance and Licensing

Every workflow must have a clear origin and a license that permits its use and
redistribution.

## Original Work

Original workflows written for this repository use `license: "Apache-2.0"`.
Set `author` to the author or publishing organisation's name.

## Adapted or Third-Party Work

For copied or adapted material, including workflows from
[`mike-workflows`](https://github.com/Open-Legal-Products/mike-workflows):

- Confirm that the upstream license permits redistribution and modification.
- Preserve all required copyright, attribution, patent, trademark, and notice
  text.
- Include a copy of every applicable third-party license under
  `third-party-licenses/` and add an entry to `NOTICE`.
- Preserve or add modification notices where required by the upstream license.
- Record the source URL, repository, revision, upstream path, author, and
  license in the workflow metadata or pack manifest.
- Keep the upstream license in the workflow's `license` field. Do not relabel
  third-party material as Apache-2.0 unless the copyright holder has granted
  that right.

MIT-licensed material from `mike-workflows` may be included in this
repository, provided its copyright notice and MIT license text are retained.
The repository-level Apache-2.0 license applies only where no more specific
license or notice applies.

Do not contribute material with unclear ownership, missing license terms, or
provenance that cannot be verified.
